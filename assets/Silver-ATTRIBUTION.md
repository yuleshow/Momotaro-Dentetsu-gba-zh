The locally supplied `Silver.ttf` is unmodified. SHA-256:

```text
5bbbcd90b9f3ed71f8e15816fc114cbfefe9dfa29e5aacab31fe3f49fe1d1c89
```

The test-ROM builder rasterizes the font into the game's 12x16, 4bpp glyph
format, adds the game's shadow pixels, and scales rasterized menu labels to
their original sprite bounds. These are adaptations of the font artwork.
Silver is used at 19 points for the native 100-unit outline grid. Missing or
oversized glyphs use the separately licensed macOS Heiti TC font, as approved
for this local test. The build report lists every fallback character.

game ROM or to the separate fallback font.
